Personalized Recovery

Investment overview

Possible supports in this investment, not a claim that every feature is included.

1A · Recovery Companion

A personal AI companion translates Arthur’s changing recovery plan into clear daily next steps through voice, visual, wearable or accessible interfaces.

1B · Recovery-Supportive Home

Arthur’s approved recovery plan becomes part of selected home routines and places, with contextual support appearing where activity occurs.

1C · Recovery Coach

AI organizes recovery information while a consistent human coach helps Arthur interpret choices, adjust routines and remain engaged.

Adaptive Rehab

Investment overview

Possible supports in this investment, not a claim that every feature is included.

2A · Guided Movement

Wearables, spatial sensing and AI adjust clinically governed movement guidance while Arthur uses the existing home.

2B · Recovery-Ready Home

The home provides more support early and allows selected support to recede or be repurposed as Arthur regains independence.

2C · Robotic Assistance

One selected wearable or environmental robotic intervention temporarily extends Arthur’s ability to perform a defined everyday task.

Early Warning

Investment overview

Possible supports in this investment, not a claim that every feature is included.

3A · Wearable Signals

Wearable and personal devices capture selected recovery indicators and identify changes that deserve attention or review.

3B · Ambient Recovery

Selected home areas observe recovery-relevant changes in function and routine with minimal active effort from Arthur.

3C · Recovery Forecast

AI aggregates legitimate available information and identifies deviation from Arthur’s individualized expected recovery trajectory.

Smart Response

Investment overview

Possible supports in this investment, not a claim that every feature is included.

4A · Right Help, Right Time

AI uses available context to identify and initiate an appropriate response pathway without requiring Arthur to navigate the system alone.

4B · Care Comes Home

For explicitly eligible episodes, Arthur’s existing home temporarily supports appropriate assessment or selected care and then returns to ordinary use.

4C · Recovery Navigator

One accountable person recognizes the unresolved need, connects Arthur to the next service, confirms receipt and follows through until responsibility is accepted.
